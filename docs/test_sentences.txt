ISL Setu — Candidate Gloss Sentences
Built entirely from the 50-word trained vocabulary
=====================================================

VOCABULARY (50 words):
I, You, He, She, We, They, Mother, Father, Friend, Teacher, Student,
School, College, Home, Hospital, Doctor, Police, Water, Food, Help,
Come, Go, Stop, Give, Take, Eat, Drink, Sleep, Work, Study, Learn,
Want, Need, Like, Know, Understand, What, Where, Who, Why, How,
Yes, No, Please, Thankyou, Sorry, Hello, Today, Tomorrow, Now

NOTE: Gloss sequences intentionally omit grammar words not in the
vocabulary (is/am/are, my/your, to, do, happen, etc.). This is
expected — Groq's job is to infer natural grammar from gloss, so the
output sentence may contain words that were never signed. That's
correct pipeline behavior, not an error.
